## Why

The existing prohibitions were written to kill the narrate-instead-of-do failure mode (the model pretending to know its own usage). Without an explicit carve-out, the guard's legitimate external-gauge read is indistinguishable from the forbidden self-introspection at review time, and Epic 3's diff would be flagged as a violation of the repo's own review criteria.
