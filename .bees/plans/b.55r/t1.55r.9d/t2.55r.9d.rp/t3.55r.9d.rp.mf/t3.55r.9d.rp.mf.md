## Context

`README.md` `### Scratch files` (lines 191-198) enumerates the residents of the workflow scratch directory and their regeneration semantics: the directory is safe to delete between runs, body files regenerate, the run-state manifest does not, and the gauge file heals itself on the producer's next refresh. After Task 2, the persistent opt-out marker is a **second non-regenerable resident**, and — unlike the manifest, whose loss only hurts mid-run — deleting it is silent and behavior-changing *across* runs: it clears the operator's standing choice.
